Account Access Policy
Password reset links expire after 30 minutes. Users can recover account access using their registered email address.

Return Policy
Unopened items may be returned within 30 days of purchase. Opened items are not eligible for return unless the product is defective.

Vacation Policy
Employees receive 15 vacation days per year. Unused vacation days cannot be carried forward unless a manager approves an exception.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week. New employees must complete their first 90 days in the office before the normal remote-work policy applies.

Learning Allowance
Employees receive a $500 learning allowance each calendar year. The policy does not state a September 30 spending deadline.
